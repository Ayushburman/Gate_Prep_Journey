# 05 · The Right Way to Study

## One Source Per Subject

Avoid collecting more books.

Depth beats variety.

---

## PYQs Are the Real Syllabus

Solve:

- by subject
- then by year

Patterns repeat.

---

## Active Recall > Rereading

Instead of rereading:

1. Close the notes.
2. Reconstruct the topic.
3. Verify.

---

## Maintain an Error Log

Track every mistake.

Categories:

- Concept gap
- Silly mistake
- Time pressure
- Guesswork

Fix the category—not just the question.

---

## NAT / MSQ Strategy

Attempt every NAT/MSQ.

No negative marking.

Still verify your reasoning.

---

## Weekly Revision

Any subject untouched for three weeks starts fading.

Rolling revision is mandatory.
