My Daily Routine

* 4–5 hours: Concept learning (one subject)
* 2 hours: PYQs from the same subject
* 1 hour: Revision of previous topics
* 30 minutes: Formula notebook / flashcards
* 30–60 minutes: Algorithms or C programming practice
