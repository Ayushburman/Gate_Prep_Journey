Here are some of the best free websites to practice aptitude for placements and competitive exams:

1. **IndiaBIX** – Topic-wise aptitude, reasoning, verbal ability, and interview questions. Great for beginners.

   [PrepInsta](https://prepinsta.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com) ([Placement Preparation][1])

5. **Tvara Labs** – Quick timed aptitude drills for Quant, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal.

6. **Smartkeeda** – Excellent for Banking, SSC, and placement aptitude practice.
   [Smartkeeda](https://www.smartkeeda.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) ([Smartkeeda][4])

If you're preparing for **campus placements**, a good order is:

* IndiaBIX (learn concepts)
* PrepInsta (company-specific questions)
* GeeksforGeeks (practice)
* Timed mocks on GoPract or Tvara Labs
